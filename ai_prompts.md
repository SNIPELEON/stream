# AI Assessment Prompts: Recycling Production Line Manager

These prompts are designed to evaluate candidates on three key competencies: Crisis Management, Sustainability Knowledge, and Team Motivation.

## System Prompt
You are an expert HR evaluator for a Recycling Facility. Your job is to assess candidate responses and provide a score from 1-10 based on the rubric provided. You must be objective, consistent, and critical.

---

## 1. Crisis Management
**Competency:** Ability to handle unexpected equipment failures and safety hazards efficiently.

**Prompt:**
> "A conveyor belt on the main sorting line has jammed, causing a pile-up of recyclable materials. Two workers are arguing about whose fault it is, and safety sensors are flashing red. Describe your immediate actions to resolve the situation, ensure safety, and get the line running again."

**Evaluation Rubric:**
- **9-10 (Exemplary):** Prioritizes safety immediately (stop line/lockout), de-escalates conflict calmly, diagnoses the technical issue, and performs a root cause analysis to prevent recurrence.
- **7-8 (Proficient):** Stops the line and ensures safety, resolves the conflict, and fixes the jam.
- **5-6 (Average):** Fixes the jam but may overlook the conflict or safety protocols.
- **1-4 (Poor):** Ignores safety, escalates conflict, or fails to fix the jam effectively.

---

## 2. Sustainability Knowledge
**Competency:** Understanding of recycling processes, contamination rates, and environmental regulations.

**Prompt:**
> "Our facility has seen a 15% increase in contamination rates in the plastic stream this month, leading to rejected bales. What steps would you take to investigate the cause and reduce contamination levels?"

**Evaluation Rubric:**
- **9-10 (Exemplary):** Proposes a data-driven approach (auditing input sources), suggests equipment calibration (optical sorters), and implements public education or supplier feedback loops.
- **7-8 (Proficient):** Suggests checking sorting equipment and retraining staff.
- **5-6 (Average):** General suggestions like "tell people to recycle better" without specific process improvements.
- **1-4 (Poor):** Blames external factors or shows lack of understanding of the sorting process.

---

## 3. Team Motivation
**Competency:** Ability to maintain high morale and productivity in a demanding physical active environment.

**Prompt:**
> "The night shift has consistently missed production targets for the last week. Morale is low, and turnover is threatening to increase. How would you address the team to identify the issue and improve performance without creating a hostile environment?"

**Evaluation Rubric:**
- **9-10 (Exemplary):** Approaches with empathy (listening sessions), identifies barriers (equipment/staffing), sets collaborative goals, and recognizes/rewards improvement.
- **7-8 (Proficient):** Talks to the team, sets clear targets, and offers some encouragement.
- **5-6 (Average):** Focuses solely on targets and discipline ("work harder").
- **1-4 (Poor):** Threatens punishment or ignores the morale issue entirely.
